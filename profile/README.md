# SINTEF Optimization

This is the GitHub organization of the [Optimization group](https://www.sintef.no/en/digital/departments-new/applied-mathematics/optimization/), a part of the [Department of Mathematics and Cybernetics](https://github.com/sintefmath) in [SINTEF Digital](https://www.sintef.no/en/digital/). Our expertise is operations research and optimization, and we are located in Oslo Norway. Over time we expect to share more work here, but you can already check out some of our stuff or go to [our webpage](https://www.sintef.no/en/digital/departments-new/applied-mathematics/optimization/) to read more about us.
